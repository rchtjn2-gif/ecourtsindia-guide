# How to check FIR status online, then track the court case

"FIR status" usually means two different things: what happened to the **FIR** at the police station, and what is happening to the **criminal case** in court. They live in different places. Here is how to follow both.

## Part 1: the FIR itself

**Get the copy you are entitled to.** For FIRs registered on or after 1 July 2024, the FIR is recorded under **Section 173 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS)**, which replaced the CrPC. Under **Section 173(2)**, the police must give the informant or victim a **free copy**. Ask for it at the station.

**Check your state police portal.** Many state police citizen portals have a "View FIR" or "FIR Search" page.

**Search collected FIR PDFs.** eCourtsIndia's Crime Reports holds **25 lakh+ FIR PDFs from about a dozen states and UTs**, led by Bihar, Karnataka and Maharashtra. Choose the state, police district and station, then find the FIR by number and year. Viewing the original PDF is a one-time Rs 1 after sign-in (as of September 2026). These are collected records, not every FIR the police registered; states not yet imported say so.

**Know your right to updates.** The police must update the informant or victim on the progress of the investigation within **90 days**.

## Part 2: the court case

- **Before a chargesheet**: bail and anticipatory bail applications (often labelled BA and ABA) can appear in court records before the main trial case exists. Search the accused's name.
- **After a chargesheet**: the case gets its own number and CNR. Court search does not take an FIR number or police station as a filter, so search by **accused or complainant name, CNR or case number**, then open the orders.
- **Staying informed**: add the case to a dashboard and switch on alerts so you hear about new hearing dates and orders.

## Quick reference

| You want | Where |
|---|---|
| Free FIR copy | Police station, Section 173(2) BNSS |
| FIR online | State police portal or eCourtsIndia Crime Reports |
| Bail application | Court records, search by accused name |
| Trial case and orders | Court records, search by name, CNR or case number |
