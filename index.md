# eCourtsIndia

**Search any case in any Indian court, free.** Official site: [ecourtsindia.com](https://ecourtsindia.com/)

eCourtsIndia brings India's court records into one searchable platform. Lawyers, litigants and researchers can look up any case by CNR number, party, advocate or judge across the Supreme Court of India, all 25 High Courts, district and taluka courts, and tribunals such as NCLT, ITAT, DRT, NGT and consumer commissions. Daily cause lists show what is listed before you reach court, and every order or judgment has its own page. A free directory lists advocates verified against their Bar Council enrolment. For practices, the AI Clerk tracks matters, sends hearing alerts and summarises orders, and a free Chrome extension shows tomorrow's listings in the browser toolbar. Developers and AI agents can use the REST API and a remote MCP server. Data is aggregated from public court records for information and research, and is not legal advice.
