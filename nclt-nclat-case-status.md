# How to check NCLT and NCLAT case status online

If you are a creditor, an employee, a homebuyer or a supplier of a company in insolvency, the case that decides your money is usually at the **National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT)**, and the appeal goes to the **National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT)**. The official portals split records by bench and keep NCLAT separately. Here is a faster method.

## The benches

- **NCLT**: a Principal Bench and 15 regional benches at 15 locations. New Delhi and Mumbai together hold about half of all matters.
- **NCLAT**: the Principal Bench in New Delhi and a bench in Chennai. NCLAT records sit under about 40 filing-office codes that begin **NCLAD** (New Delhi) or **NCLAC/NCLAE** (Chennai).

## Four ways to find a matter on eCourtsIndia

eCourtsIndia indexes every NCLT bench and NCLAT in the same free search as the Supreme Court, High Courts and district courts, with **2.5 lakh+ NCLT matters and 70,000+ NCLAT matters** (October 2026). Search is free, needs no login and has no paid ranking.

1. **Company name**: pick the bench and search the corporate debtor's name.
2. **CNR**: paste the 16-character CNR to open the case directly.
3. **Bench code**: open a bench directly with its court code, for example `NCLTMB0` for Mumbai or `NCLTDL0` for New Delhi.
4. **Free text inside orders**: the search box reads inside the full text of uploaded orders, not just the case header, so you can search a resolution professional's name or a financial creditor.

**Only have the tribunal's e-filing number?** eCourtsIndia's tribunal CNR generator converts it into a CNR you can search.

## Case types you will see

The largest is **IA_IBC**, interlocutory applications under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, about three for every petition that starts an insolvency. These include claims disputes, avoidance applications and applications about the resolution plan, so a single insolvency can produce dozens of linked matters. Read them, not just the main petition.

## Following a matter up the chain

A matter that starts at NCLT Mumbai, goes to NCLAT New Delhi and then to the Supreme Court can be followed in one search, because all three sit in the same index.
